WHO WE ARE

Built with Purpose. Led with Heart.

Genesis & Co. Legacy Services was founded in 2025 by Kam , a passionate wife and mother with a lifelong desire to help others. After years of experience in customer service and healthcare, Kam knew the next chapter of her life needed to center around what she loved most— connecting with people, helping others, and finding solutions.

With that vision in mind, she pursued the education, training, and certifications needed to turn that passion into purpose and founded Genesis & Co. Legacy Services.

As a stay-at-home mother, Kam also understands the convenience, flexibility, and value of having trusted solutions all in one place. Genesis & Co. was created with that in mind—a place where individuals and families can access a variety of professional services designed to help them protect, prepare, and move forward with confidence.

Today, Kam is grateful for every individual and family who chooses Genesis & Co. Legacy Services. It is more than a business—it is an opportunity to serve, build relationships, and make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

Thank you for allowing Genesis & Co. to be a part of your journey.
